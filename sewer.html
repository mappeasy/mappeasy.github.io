How sewer, parcel, CIP, and depth are selected
The app matches the address to a nearby HCAD parcel using the house number and location, then checks whether the snapped gravity-sewer segment intersects that parcel. Per the current business rule, intersecting = Backlot and non-intersecting = Non-Backlot. Wastewater CIP is preferred as the primary CIP match. For depth, the app checks the snapped sewer endpoints, then traces upstream before using the fallback depth.
